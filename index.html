New campaign

Five steps. You'll be done in ten minutes.

Discover

Creators who actually posted in your niche this week — with a contact where one is public. Anyone you've already reached is filtered out automatically.

Lists

Discover results you saved — kept in the cloud, classified by AI into niches and audience tiers, so a search from months ago is still there and still useful, on any device.

Match

Describe the campaign. Cadence ranks every creator in your index against it and tells you what the money buys.

Outreach

One message, sent one at a time inside a window you choose — the only sending pattern that does not look like a blast.

Pipeline

Every creator Cadence is handling, from first contact to a video that actually carries your link — one AI pass over the whole list instead of a person per stage.

Copilot

One Sonnet agent that every feature reports to — discovery, outreach, inbox, pipeline, campaigns and payouts. It sorts the stream, reacts as things happen, keeps working on the server while this tab is closed, and hands you only what needs a decision.
