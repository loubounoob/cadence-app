FAQ

Quick answers.

Couldn't we do this ourselves with AI?

AI can give you a list of names. It won't get creators to reply, get them excited, collect addresses, chase each one until the post is live, or check that the views are real. That part is the job, and it's ours.

Does this replace our ads?

No. Creators bring you a new audience. Your ads convert it better.

Who pays the creators?

Your product does. One package per creator, no fees.

What do you guarantee?

At least 80% of the creators you approve post within the window. If not, we replace them or refund the gap. Views depend on creators and content: we track and report every one, but we don't promise a number.

What does it cost?

It depends on your brand, your goals and your market. We build every campaign to cost less than buying the same reach with ads. Apply and we'll come back to you within 48 hours.

Which platforms?

TikTok and Instagram Reels.

How fast can we start?

About 2 weeks after approval.
